I am an International A Level student at Heritage School, Narayanganj, Bangladesh, with interests in biology, medicine, mathematics, and physics. My interest in biomedical research has led me to work with the Human Genetics Research and Training Center (HGRTC), contributing to HPV research and presenting related work at EUROGIN 2026 in Vienna, Austria.

Beyond research, I enjoy teaching mathematics and leading community-focused projects, including the Heritage Pathshala Sustainability Initiative. I hope to continue exploring how science, quantitative thinking, and research can address meaningful real-world problems.
